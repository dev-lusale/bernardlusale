About

I build technology for the conditions people actually live in.

I'm a Computer Science student at Copperbelt University in Kitwe, Zambia. Most of my work starts from a local problem: teachers short on preparation time, languages that mainstream software ignores, vehicles that lose satellite positioning, mining communities exposed to environmental risk.

Those problems come with constraints that shape every design decision: low-end devices, limited bandwidth and patchy connectivity. Working within them is the part I enjoy most. It's where artificial intelligence and data stop being buzzwords and become tools that have to earn their place.

Alongside my degree I founded Kalulu Vision, co-founded Busaka Enviroworks, lead the ICTAZ Copperbelt University chapter and compete in hackathons and project contests, at home and abroad.

    05

    Research & Interests

    The questions I want to spend my career on.

    Can useful AI run where the internet doesn't, and speak the languages people actually use?

    1. Natural language processing for African languages

      How can translation and language tools for Bemba and Nyanja work offline on everyday phones, with very little training data?

      Connected work: TalkZed · Indigenous Languages Framework

    2. Computer vision for accessibility

      What would it take to recognise Zambian Sign Language in real time, and how do you build the first dataset responsibly?

      Connected work: SignBridge

    3. Intelligent systems under uncertainty

      How far can probabilistic methods and prior knowledge, such as road maps, carry a system when its main sensors fail?

      Connected work: MCDR

    4. Big data and analytics for public value

      How can large public datasets, from procurement records to agriculture and youth employment data, become evidence decision-makers actually use?

      Connected work: Evoke Hackathon · public-data analyses

    5. AI and machine learning in education

      Can retrieval-grounded AI reduce teachers' preparation load without drifting away from the national curriculum?

      Connected work: Educom AI

    6. Applied research and innovation

      How do you turn a research prototype into something an institution, a company or a community can adopt?

      Connected work: Kalulu Vision · Busaka Enviroworks
